When does a structured shell help a coding agent?

URL:
https://3cucharadas.cl/en/ia/productividad/desarrollo/nushell-coprocesador-estructurado/?utm_source=hackernews&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=nushell-coprocesador-estructurado

PRIMER COMENTARIO DEL AUTOR:
I expected the structured route to mainly reduce brittle parsing. The result was less tidy: it improved observed accuracy on some task families, cost time and output tokens, and made no accuracy difference in an aggregate case based on my master's thesis. The largest limitation is that the task families are few and some were used during tuning, so I added a holdout and report the tuned result separately. I would be interested in stronger baselines or counterexamples, especially from people who route between shell tools rather than replacing one shell with another.
